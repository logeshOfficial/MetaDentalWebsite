# External image sources

The treatment cards primarily use photographs supplied by META DENTAL. The following cards use external images because the clinic library did not contain a distinct, relevant photograph:

- **Dentures:** [Denture Example 1](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Denture_Example_1.jpg), released into the public domain by DRosenbach.
- **Kids’ dentistry:** [Photo of a Child in Dental Clinic](https://www.pexels.com/photo/photo-of-a-child-in-dental-clinic-8224633/) by Lakhinandan Borah, photographed in Assam, India and used under the Pexels licence.
- **Root canal treatment:** [An Indian dentist at work](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:An_Indian_dentist_at_work.jpg) by Frederick Noronha, photographed in Goa, India and used under CC BY-SA 4.0.

The downloaded files were cropped, resized and converted to WebP for the treatment-card layout.
